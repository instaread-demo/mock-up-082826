Second only to Russ & Daughters’ heavenly menu is its clean, vintage design. Step under the green and red neon sign on Houston Street or into the mid-century modern cafe on Orchard, and you just know storied excellence awaits.

Thankfully, you can now bring a slice of this Jewish appetizing institution home — and it’s guaranteed to last longer than a chocolate babka!

Today, Russ & Daughters and West Elm released their homeware line collaboration. The stoneware collection features everything a balaboosta needs: dinner and entertaining sets, serving platters, trinket trays, cutting boards for bagels and challah, a tea light holder and even a menorah. All of it comes in Russ & Daughter’s iconic blue and white palette.

Plus, if you order by September 4, 2026 at 6 a.m. PT/9 a.m. ET, you can get everything in time for Rosh Hashanah! Happy shopping.
