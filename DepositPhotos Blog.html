10 Best AI Tools for Brainstorming Creative Ideas in 2026

Whether you’re working on a marketing campaign, social media content, or a product description, that aha moment is often what leads to something bigger. AI brainstorming tools can help you move from a blank page to actionable concepts.

These AI-powered software solutions go beyond providing ready-made answers. They help you explore, organize, and refine your ideas while guiding you through multiple concepts until you find the one that best fits your needs. From clustering thoughts on digital whiteboards to generating visuals, such tools offer a wide range of features to support brainstorming workflows. Here’s our pick of the best AI brainstorming tools to accelerate your creative process.

TL;DR

AI accelerates ideation by turning raw concepts into structured, high-impact workflows. Combining text-based generators with visual platforms yields the strongest results, but the right choice ultimately depends on your team’s specific output:

  • Best overall for visual brainstorming: DepositPhotos AI Assistant (for visual styles, mood boards, and reference sourcing)
  • Best overall for general ideation: ChatGPT (for strategy development, broad topic expansion, and messaging)
  • Best overall for brand marketing: Jasper (for campaign briefs, promotional copy, and brand voice alignment)

What to look for in an AI brainstorming tool

When evaluating an AI brainstorming tool, prioritize solutions that balance rapid concept generation with flexible workflow integration. This balance consistently yields more creative ideas while maintaining team efficiency and momentum.

Idea generation

The ability to help a team develop a rough concept into a stronger idea is what makes an AI-powered brainstorming tool truly effective. A smart tool prompts relevant questions to ensure you don’t hit a creative wall while keeping focus on the core concept. 

Platforms that support iterative prompting allow users to expand their thoughts, explore adjacent angles, and refine them into practical solutions.

Visual ideation

Text-based brainstorming has limitations, especially during extended ideation sessions. According to a recent study in Creativity and Innovation Management, integrated visual inspiration helps teams overcome creative blocks, approach problems from new angles, and maintain ideation velocity. Visualization keeps the creative process moving, even when it feels like you’ve been circling a single idea.

Built-in libraries of high-quality images and generative visuals enable teams to create mood boards for ads, marketing campaigns, brand packaging, and visual storytelling.

Collaboration & workflow integration

Bringing colleagues together presents unique organizational challenges. AI-powered tools with real-time collaboration features empower distributed teams to exchange ideas effortlessly, while structured frameworks like the AI sandwich approach, as highlighted by executive consultancy Russell Reynolds Associates, help balance human ingenuity with automated assistance.  while structured 

Creative automation and seamless tool integrations are equally crucial. They keep team members up to speed in real time, reducing the need for frequent sync meetings or repetitive Slack updates. 

Whether you want to improve brainstorming efficiency or future-proof your team’s ideation process, the following tools can help you make an informed decision.

The best AI brainstorming tools at a glance

To select the best AI brainstorming tools, we evaluated platforms on five key criteria: idea generation speed, iterative refinement, software integration, role specialization, and real-time collaboration. We prioritized tools that rapidly turn simple prompts into actionable concepts tailored to specific workflows—like visual design, diagramming, or copywriting.

1. Depositphotos AI Assistant 

DepositPhotos AI Assistant is a chat-based creative agent that helps users develop initial ideas into visual concepts. It combines brainstorming support with stock content discovery, AI image generation, and visual editing through natural-language conversations. For marketing and creative teams, this means quickly exploring new campaign directions, sourcing matching stock assets, and building custom visuals for commercial projects.

Key features 

  • Creative ideation and concept exploration
  • AI-powered search for stock images, videos, and music 
  • Integrated AI image generation models (Nano Banana and Bria)
  • Reference image search and inspiration
  • AI-powered image editing (background removal, image upscaling, object removal, and more)

Pros

  • Seamlessly connects early-stage brainstorming, stock asset discovery, and AI generation without tool-switching.
  • Provides access to licensed stock content alongside AI-generated visuals for commercial use.
  • Helps refine specific visual elements like style, color mood, and composition.

Cons

  • Less suitable for general business planning or text-only research.
  • English prompts currently produce the most precise image generation results, although the Assistant supports multiple languages.

2. ChatGPT

ChatGPT is a versatile ideation assistant that helps teams generate, structure, and refine ideas across writing, marketing, and strategy. It supports both early-stage concept exploration and later-stage idea development, helping users turn rough thoughts into more structured frameworks and actionable plans.

Key features

  • AI-powered idea generation and refinement
  • Concept development and alternative angle suggestions
  • Structured brainstorming with lists, frameworks, outlines, and action plans
  • Visual inspiration through reference image analysis

Pros

  • Highly versatile across teams and workflows
  • Supports rapid concept exploration and ideation 
  • Accessible and easy to use for all skill levels

Cons

  • Not specialized for production-ready visuals, requiring dedicated design tools for advanced graphic workflows
  • Output quality depends heavily on prompt precision, with vague inputs more likely to produce generic ideas

3. Gamma

Gamma is a presentation-first AI platform that helps users turn raw ideas into engaging decks, campaign proposals, creative briefs, and web pages without requiring advanced design skills. Its collaborative workspace allows teams to structure content, refine messaging, and adapt formats in real time.

Key Features

  • Generates presentations and other visual formats from text prompts
  • Transforms unstructured ideas into clear visual narratives
  • Automatically creates layouts, copy, and supporting visual elements
  • Offers real-time AI editing and inline prompt suggestions to refine content

Pros

  • Delivers polished, visually engaging outputs with minimal effort
  • Reduces time spent on formatting and manual design
  • Easy to edit, adapt across formats, and share through interactive links

Cons

  • Less suited for open-ended, freeform brainstorming compared to AI ideation tools
  • Offers less granular design control and pixel-level customization than traditional graphic software

4. Miro AI 

Having recently shifted to an AI-first strategy, Miro provides an expansive digital workspace where distributed teams can organize concepts visually and streamline collaborative discussions. Its AI-driven mind mapping, diagramming, and whiteboarding features accelerate real-time idea generation, helping turn scattered thoughts into actionable project plans.

Key features

  • Generates mind maps from prompts
  • Automatically organizes sticky notes and brainstormed ideas
  • Creates wireframes, workflows, diagrams, and flowcharts
  • Helps teams structure and visualize ideas on a shared canvas
  • Summarizes brainstorming sessions and extracts key takeaways

Pros

  • Excellent for collaborative idea generation
  • Flexible visual workspace with a spacious canvas
  • AI helps organize large brainstorming sessions
  • Integrates with workplace tools, including Slack, Jira, and Microsoft Teams

Cons

  • Steeper learning curve due to its extensive feature set
  • Can feel overwhelming for users who need a simple, focused brainstorming tool

5. Notion AI

Notion AI seamlessly connects ideation with documentation, helping teams transform raw thoughts into content calendars, project roadmaps, and knowledge bases. It excels at organizing scattered concepts, proposing actionable next steps, and managing entire creative workflows within a single hub.

Key features

  • Generates article and campaign ideas
  • Summarizes brainstorming notes and meeting discussions
  • Organizes projects within Notion workspaces
  • Creates outlines from rough ideas
  • Expands rough drafts into more developed content

Pros

  • Fits naturally into existing content workflows
  • Keeps brainstorming, documentation, and project planning in one place
  • Helps maintain long-term project organization
  • Well suited to structured content planning

Cons

  • Large workspaces can occasionally experience slower performance
  • Limited offline functionality

6. Whimsical AI 

Whimsical AI is a prompt-based visual tool designed for flowcharting, mind mapping, and structural diagramming. It transforms raw concepts into clean, actionable frameworks—making it particularly useful for marketing planning, user journey mapping, and organizing complex ideas. Its intuitive interface allows users to dive straight into visual brainstorming with minimal onboarding.

Key features

  • Generates flowcharts from text prompts
  • Creates mind maps with real-time collaboration
  • Builds user journey maps for products and customers
  • Creates AI-assisted wireframes
  • Provides an interactive AI-powered canvas for visual idea generation

Pros

  • Clean, intuitive interface
  • Well suited to structured visual thinking
  • Easy sharing via links
  • Fast creation with integrated AI

Cons

  • Less suitable for large-scale, open-ended brainstorming
  • Fewer capabilities for complex team workflows than broader collaboration platforms

7. Ideanote AI 

Ideanote is an AI-powered innovation management platform designed to capture, evaluate, and develop ideas across an organization. It uses AI to collect and evaluate suggestions from employees or customers, identify patterns, and enrich concepts with actionable insights. The platform is primarily focused on collaborative innovation but can also support individual idea development.

Key features

  • Scores and prioritizes ideas
  • Identifies trends and connections between ideas
  • Tracks ideas through development stages
  • Integrates with Zapier
  • Supports collaborative idea evaluation

Pros

  • Well suited to structured innovation programs
  • Manages ideas across multiple development stages
  • Supports repeatable innovation workflows
  • Offers templates for different innovation initiatives

Cons

  • Less appealing to individual creators
  • More focused on business innovation than everyday creative brainstorming

8. Stormboard AI 

Stormboard AI provides an interactive digital whiteboard tailored to enterprise workshops, agile planning, and remote team collaboration. It helps distributed teams organize complex discussions, prioritize key ideas, and turn AI-assisted brainstorming sessions into actionable project workflows.

Key features

  • Organizes and manages sticky notes for structured brainstorming
  • Helps create workflows for different business processes
  • Provides customizable templates for different workshop formats
  • Supports voting and idea prioritization
  • Enables remote workshops with shared boards and team interaction tools

Pros

  • Excellent for structured team workshops
  • Useful for distributed teams
  • Flexible enough for different workshop formats and workflows
  • Keeps brainstorming results organized in one place

Cons

  • Primarily geared toward larger teams and structured workshops
  • Less suited to quick, informal solo brainstorming

9. FigJam AI 

FigJam AI is a collaborative whiteboard platform from Figma that helps designers and product teams generate concepts, structure UX discussions, and run interactive workshops. It accelerates early-stage product development by turning freeform visual brainstorming into organized, actionable design workflows.

Key features

  • Generates ready-to-use brainstorming prompts
  • Organizes and summarizes sticky notes
  • Provides Jambot for ideation, prompts, and creative exercises
  • Integrates with Figma design projects
  • Supports design sprints and workshop activities

Pros

  • Smooth real-time collaboration
  • Useful facilitation tools for results-oriented meetings
  • Strong visual tools for organizing and connecting ideas
  • Rich brainstorming toolkit, including connectors, custom drawings, and chat features

Cons

  • Large, complex boards can become harder to manage
  • Less suitable for general business planning and non-design workflows

10. Jasper

Jasper is an AI copilot built specifically for marketing teams to generate campaign concepts, messaging strategies, and brand-aligned content across channels. It helps teams establish creative direction, draft structured content briefs, and refine promotional copy while maintaining consistency with established brand guidelines.

Key features

  • Generates campaign concepts
  • Brainstorms marketing angles
  • Produces content briefs
  • Creates and adapts content across different formats
  • Applies brand guidelines to generated content

Pros

  • Strong campaign ideation and development
  • Helps maintain consistency with brand guidelines
  • Easy-to-navigate user interface
  • Well suited to marketing-specific workflows

Cons

  • Less versatile for general-purpose brainstorming outside marketing
  • Limited value for visual or non-marketing ideation

FAQs

How does AI help with brainstorming?

AI helps with brainstorming by providing creative sparks, guiding user thinking, asking probing follow-up questions, and expanding raw concepts. AI-powered software solutions accelerate idea generation, allowing teams to move faster from concept to execution.

How do you brainstorm with AI effectively?

Effective AI brainstorming requires giving the tool a specific persona, clear project constraints, and detailed context rather than asking broad questions. Treat the AI’s initial output as a starting draft, using follow-up prompts to challenge generic ideas or request alternative angles. 

How can businesses maximize AI brainstorming tools?

Start with clear goals—whether generating campaign ideas, structuring project workflows, or exploring visual directions. Treat AI as a collaborative thought partner, combining machine-generated insights with human expertise to produce original, practical results. 

Which AI tool is best for brainstorming marketing campaigns?

Jasper is the top choice for copy-driven marketing, while the DepositPhotos AI Assistant leads for visual campaigns. Jasper excels at creating brand-aligned messaging and content briefs across multi-channel campaigns. DepositPhotos AI Assistant pairs creative concepts with licensed stock assets, while ChatGPT helps generate broad strategic hooks.

Which AI brainstorming tool is best for designers?

FigJam AI is the top choice for UI/UX product designers, while the DepositPhotos AI Assistant is ideal for visual and graphic designers. FigJam AI provides interactive whiteboarding and design sprint tools that integrate directly into Figma pipelines. Meanwhile, the DepositPhotos AI Assistant helps designers build mood boards, analyze reference images, and explore visual styles effortlessly.

Can I use several AI brainstorming tools? 

Yes, combining AI brainstorming tools often yields the best outcomes. You can use a conversational assistant to explore ideas, a whiteboard tool to structure them, and a visual AI platform to explore creative directions or develop supporting imagery.

How to use AI for brainstorming content ideas?

To use AI for brainstorming content ideas effectively, start by giving the tool clear context about your project format, target audience, and primary goal. For instance, the DepositPhotos AI Assistant prompts for these exact parameters—along with your desired tone—to immediately generate tailored visual directions and design concepts. Defining these details upfront ensures the AI delivers focused, actionable ideas rather than generic output.

What is the difference between text-based and visual AI brainstorming tools?

Text-based tools (like ChatGPT or Jasper) generate campaign messaging, topic outlines, and strategic frameworks. Visual AI tools (like the DepositPhotos AI Assistant or Miro AI) focus on spatial ideation, mapping user flows, and establishing visual directions like color palettes and style references.
