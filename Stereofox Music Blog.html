I just wrapped up my EP review of Aloefi and Cuezy.'s brand-new 6-track EP, Endless Summer, and as tradition goes - it's time to do a single spotlight in our Stereofox music feed.

Meet "Give Up Everything" - a track that sounds like Kaytranada spent a sun-soaked summer on the Sri Lankan coast, making groovy lofi beats between ocean swims, late lunches, and golden-hour sessions.

"Our mutual love for hip hop culture, digging for records, beatmaking, and the close relationship between hip hop and soulful house flows through every track. Soulful boom bap drums, dusty lofi textures, jazz-influenced instrumentation, warm house grooves, and expressive vocal performances come together to create a sound that feels nostalgic while remaining fresh and contemporary," Robert and Nicolas elaborate.

For more, stop by my review. You won't regret it.
