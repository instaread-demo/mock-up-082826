The Minnesota Vikings drafted CB Mekhi Blackmon out of USC in the third round of the 2023 NFL Draft. He joined his USC teammate, who was drafted in the first round by Minnesota, Jordan Addison.

Blackmon had a promising rookie season in 2023, and it looked like he had the tools to develop into a decent rotational corner for the future. Unfortunately, going into his second season, he suffered a season-ending injury in July before the season started, which robbed him of a pivotal developmental year. Apparently, in 2025, the Vikings weren’t loving what they were seeing upon his return and traded him to the Indianapolis Colts.

The trade netted Minnesota a 2026 sixth-round selection, with the Colts deciding it was worth the price to take a shot on a young corner whose stock had lowered a bit after a major injury.

Blackmon didn’t do anything special, and now he currently sits without employment.

Former Vikings CB Mekhi Blackmon Released by Indianapolis

The Colts announced on Wednesday that they released Mekhi Blackmon and signed CB Kenneth Harris.

If Colts ESPN reporter Stephen Holder is anyone to trust, then it’s quite clear why Blackmon got the boot. “The Colts have waived CB Mekhi Blackmon, replacing him with CB Kenneth Harris,” shared Holder. “Blackmon played 67% of the defensive snaps last season. Many of those snaps were ROUGH.”

Kevin Bowen of 1075 The Fan shared that “No Colts cornerback played more snaps than Blackmon last season. But he was struggling this preseason.”

Mekhi Blackmon’s Fall from “Grace”

Mekhi Blackmon didn’t light the world on fire or anything during his rookie season for the Vikings, but it is a bit surprising to see just how poorly he played in his first year back from injury, as well as this preseason. He looked like a corner that could at least appear on the field in a rotational role for Minnesota, but now, he’s not even lasting on rosters until the big cutdown day after the preseason’s conclusion.

Blackmon still has a decent shot to be picked up to a team’s practice squad in the coming season, so not all hope is completely lost for his professional football career.
