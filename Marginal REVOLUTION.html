The Antitrust Academy

by  Alex Tabarrok August 27, 2026 at 7:17 am in
  • Economics
  • Law
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by

Instaread

The Antitrust Academy is an online platform hosting hundreds of videos comprising a complete course in antitrust law and economics. Teachers include Judge Douglas Ginsburg, Jon Klick, Joshua Wright and others. I am an advisor. A great resource whether you are learning this material for the first time or need to brush up on some legal or economic doctrines.

From a business standpoint, George Mason University (GMU), the Mercatus Center, and Scalia Law School act as an intellectual shield for big business. While they preach about "free markets," they actually run a clever operation that turns corporate donations into legal protection.For tech giants like Google and Amazon, antitrust lawsuits are incredibly expensive. To protect their profits, they give millions to GMU. In return, GMU produces complex economic theories that say big corporations are good for consumers and should be left alone.The school then invites federal judges to free, all-expenses-paid "economics bootcamps." At these events, judges are taught to view government regulation as bad and corporate monopolies as efficient.The irony is obvious. These institutions claim to love open competition, but they take money from corporate giants to build a legal system that protects those same giants from ever having to compete fairly.
