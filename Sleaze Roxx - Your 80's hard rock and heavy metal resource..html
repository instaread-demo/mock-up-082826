Aussie metal rockers Tyrant featuring lead vocalist Neil “Steel’ Wilson will be uploading their only live release, Live Bootleg (2005), on YouTube in about one week’s time.

The following message was posted on Tyrant‘s Facebook page on August 18, 2026:

“TYRANT‘S ‘SOLD OUT’ ALBUM – “LIVE BOOTLEG” TO BE UPLOADED…

Veteran old-school heavy metal band – TYRANT are to upload the band’s only official LIVE recording, “Live Bootleg” in 2 weeks time!

The 7 song album will be a ‘digital’ release and was recorded in mid 2004 via Hobart radio station, Edge Radio 99.3 FM – LIVE broadcast at the University of Tasmania, situated in the Hobart suburb of Sandy Bay.

Engineered by the Edge Radio CEO Chris Johnson, the through-the-desk recording captures the 5-piece metal band tearing through their set of songs, with bruising renditions of some fan favourites, including an absolute crushing version of ‘Thunder Down Under’!

The line up is:

Nathan Sprouley – drums
Jason Smith – bass guitars, vocals
Ziggypsy – guitars, vocals
Brett Collidge – guitars, vocals
Neil “Steel” Wilson – vocals.

“LIVE BOOTLEG” was released as a ‘limited’ edition CD release in 2005 and SOLD OUT within 6 months and has never been reissued since!

“LIVE BOOTLEG” Set List:
1. Killing Machine
2. Freaks Of Nature
3. Wicked Game (Chris Issac Cover)
4. Better Off Dead*
5. Dressed To Kill
6. Lamb To The Slaughter
7. Thunder Down Under

* The song ‘Better Off Dead’ has been uploaded to the band’s YouTube page as an audio release!”
