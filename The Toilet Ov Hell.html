    This week on Toilet Radio: Maynard James Keenan wants to make you eat sushi in the desert. The guy from Skindred has one million grandchildren. King 810 is threatening to release a new album and tour the rust belt – what exactly happened to these guys? Snot is trying again and 90s funk metal will never die. Demon Hunter is suing the studio behind K-POP Demon Hunter and we take a deep dive into K-Pop, anime, and Christian metal. Charley Crockett is STILL trying to beef with Twin Temple and Jack White, who are getting huge boosts from Charley acting like a fucking freak online. Finally, Jack Osbourne is teasing a new Ozzfest – this time it’ll be a one-off somewhere in England. Weak. FOLKS….. it’s a good one.
